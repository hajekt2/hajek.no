## Work

I'm a co-founder, managing consultant, and lead architect at [Xper Consulting AS](https://xperconsulting.no), a Norwegian consultancy focused on Customer Communication Management, document composition, accessibility, and document automation.

I help companies generate, transform, validate, and deliver documents that must be correct every time. Much of that work involves old templates, business rules, batch processing, archives, delivery channels, and integrations that cannot be replaced casually.

At Xper we combine consulting with product development. We are building DocEngine to add accessibility structure to existing PDFs, validate the result, and generate accessible PDF and HTML from templates and structured input data.

I'm also building [CCMForge](https://ccmforge.com). It brings template analysis, validation, publishing, HTML/PDF composition, and Exstream-focused assistance into one document engineering workflow.

## Projects

### [CCMForge](https://ccmforge.com)

[CCMForge](https://ccmforge.com) is where I am turning years of CCM work into tools. The platform covers template lifecycle work, validation, publishing, HTML/PDF composition, and Exstream-focused assistance.

### [UA1.dev](https://ua1.dev)

[UA1.dev](https://ua1.dev) is a free PDF/UA-1 validator. You can drop a PDF into the web interface for a quick check or call the JSON API from a CI pipeline, script, or AI agent.

I built it because accessibility checks belong close to document production. Teams should be able to run the same validation repeatedly instead of waiting for a final manual check.
