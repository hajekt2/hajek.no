## Work

I'm a co-founder, managing consultant, and lead architect at [Xper Consulting AS](https://xperconsulting.no), a Norwegian consultancy focused on Customer Communication Management, document composition, accessibility, and document automation.

I help companies generate, transform, validate, and deliver documents that must be correct every time. Much of that work involves old templates, business rules, batch jobs, archives, delivery channels, and integrations that cannot be replaced casually.

At Xper we combine consulting with product development. We are building DocEngine to add accessibility structure to existing PDFs and validate the result. It also generates accessible PDF and HTML from templates and structured data.

I'm also building [CCMForge](https://ccmforge.com). It brings template analysis, validation, publishing, HTML/PDF composition, and Exstream-focused assistance into one document engineering workflow.

## Projects

### [CCMForge](https://ccmforge.com)

[CCMForge](https://ccmforge.com) turns problems I have met in CCM projects into tools. It covers template analysis, validation, publishing, HTML/PDF composition, and Exstream-specific help.

### [UA1.dev](https://ua1.dev)

[UA1.dev](https://ua1.dev) is a free PDF/UA-1 validator. You can drop a PDF into the web interface for a quick check or call the JSON API from a CI pipeline, script, or AI agent.

I built it because accessibility checks belong close to document production. Teams need to run the same check after every change, not wait for one manual check at the end.
